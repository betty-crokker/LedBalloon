# Code of conduct

This is a small project about making a house look nice. The bar is not complicated.

## What is expected

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to get something to work, and
answer the question they asked.

Disagreement about the code is the point of the exercise and is welcome — including disagreement
with the maintainer, and including the unglamorous kind where somebody drives a controller and shows
that a confident claim in a comment is wrong. That has happened more than once and the comments were
rewritten. Being shown to be wrong about a thing is not an attack on the person who was wrong.

## What is not

Harassment, insults, sustained disruption, publishing someone's private information, and sexual
attention or imagery. Also: dismissing somebody because of who they are rather than what they wrote.

None of that becomes acceptable for being technically correct at the same time.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to Chris Cooper through a GitHub issue if it can be discussed openly, or by email at
**led_balloon_app@proton.me** if it cannot. Reports are handled privately, and the person reporting is not expected to confront anybody
first.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block accounts. Where
that happens for conduct rather than content, it will be said so plainly rather than dressed up as a
technical decision.

## Scope

This applies in the issues, the pull requests, the commit log, and anywhere somebody is representing
the project.

---

This is a plain-language code of conduct rather than an adopted standard text. If you would prefer
the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), its
terms are compatible with everything above and nothing here is intended to narrow them.
